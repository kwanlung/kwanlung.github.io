## How I use this notebook

I aim to document the process, not just the outcome: the question I started with, the resources I studied, what I built or tested, what failed, and what I will try next.

The writing will mostly be in English as a way to practise explaining technical and non-technical ideas clearly. These notes are personal learning records, not professional financial advice or a claim to have all the answers.
